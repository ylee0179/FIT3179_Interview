A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Five things to know before exploring

A few useful terms to guide you through the collection.

01

Specimen

One catalogued rock or crystal kept in the museum.

02

Mineral species

A mineral defined by its chemistry and crystal structure.

03

Mineral family

A group of minerals sorted by chemistry, such as carbonates or silicates.

04

Crystal system

The shape of the tiny repeating building block inside a crystal.

05

Mohs hardness

A scratch test from 1 (softest) to 10 (hardest).

How the data was prepared

Countries, states and mineral names come from catalogue locality fields; some origins are unknown. Collecting towns were matched to approximate coordinates. Mineral families come from International Mineralogical Association chemistry. Densities divide counts by land area. Distances to 2025 mines are straight-line and illustrative. Museum records were last updated in July 2016.
